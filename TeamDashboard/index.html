How tracking works

Assign any tracked role in Discord to add a member. Refresh once Discord has sent the role change to the tracker. Current members appear even before they record activity.

Activity is assigned to roles held when it happened. A person who leaves a role can still appear in an earlier quarter. All roles counts each person's activity once.

An active day means at least one message or one minute of shared voice. Shared voice counts time with at least two non-deafened humans in the channel, including the member being tracked. Total voice includes solo and deafened time. The tracker excludes the designated AFK channel.

Quarters begin January 1, April 1, July 1, and October 1 in Eastern time. Select a past quarter to see its retained figures. Export CSV to keep reports beyond the tracker’s 400-day history.
