Quarterly tracking & site owner guide

Quarters follow Eastern time: Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, and Oct–Dec. The current quarter changes automatically. Selecting a new quarter starts its figures fresh without deleting previous activity.

  1. Edit content progress in the creator’s quarterProgress(longForm, shorts) entry inside this HTML.
  2. Record confirmed event attendance in COMMUNITY_EVENTS, using a separate YYYY-QN entry for each quarter. Unrecorded attendance is not treated as zero.
  3. Keep creator-to-Discord links in DISCORD_USERS. Names can change; Discord IDs remain the same.
  4. Discord history begins when the tracker starts. Earlier days and connection gaps are not assumed to be inactivity. Daily records are retained for 400 days; export completed quarters to CSV for longer-term records.
