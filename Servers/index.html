Common questions

FaithForge Servers FAQ

Quick answers for players searching for a Christian gaming server, family-friendly multiplayer community, Minecraft crossplay server, PvE survival server, or D&D group.

What game servers does FaithForge Gaming host?
FaithForge Gaming offers Minecraft Multiverse, All The Mods 9, a Lord of the Rings Minecraft server, Valheim, Palworld, ARK: Survival Ascended and ARK: Survival Evolved PvE servers, plus Dungeons & Dragons campaigns and one-shots.
Is FaithForge Gaming a Christian and family-friendly gaming community?
Yes. FaithForge Gaming is a Christ-centered, family-friendly community with respectful conduct expectations and profanity-free community standards.
Can Minecraft Java and Bedrock players join the FaithForge Multiverse?
Yes. The FaithForge Minecraft Multiverse supports Java and Bedrock crossplay through Geyser and Floodgate.
How do I join a FaithForge game server?
Join the FaithForge Gaming Discord, read the community rules, select the role for the game you want to play, and follow that game's connection or whitelist instructions.
Does the FaithForge Minecraft server have pay-to-win features?
The FaithForge Minecraft Multiverse is built with a no-pay-to-win approach.
Can new players join the FaithForge Dungeons & Dragons campaign?
Yes. New players are welcome to join ongoing FaithForge Dungeons & Dragons adventures. Shadows of Redemption uses an in-game portal system so characters can enter and exit as needed.
