FaithForge Gaming Creator Partnership Program

One Team. One Standard.

Track quarterly content, community involvement, merch eligibility, live Twitch status, and the strongest creator metrics currently available from FaithForge analytics.

6 + 1

Quarterly Content

Six short-form videos and one quality long-form video every quarter. Long-form work should be intentionally edited and structured, not a raw VOD or minimally edited clip.

Show Up

Community Participation

Make a genuine effort to attend as many FFG events as reasonably possible. Consistent involvement matters more than a strict monthly attendance number.

1 Quarter

Merch Store Access

Every streamer has the same opportunity. Merch store eligibility begins after successfully completing the first full quarter and meeting Stream Team expectations.

Christ First

Content & Conduct

Content does not have to be strictly family-friendly, but speech, behavior, interactions, promotions, and presentation should always be Christ-honoring.

Site owner update guide
All manual updates are grouped in one clearly marked section near the top of the main script.
  1. Update progress by changing only the two numbers inside quarterProgress(longForm, shorts).
  2. Add a creator by copying the included creator template and changing the profile details.
  3. Start a new quarter by adding one quarter entry and changing DEFAULT_QUARTER_ID. Previous-quarter progress stays available in the quarter selector.
