The heart of partnership

A Christian stream team
built around community.

FaithForge brings Christian streamers and gaming content creators together around faith, friendship, and service. Whether you create on Twitch, YouTube, or through gaming videos and social content, your presence matters here.

Our Creator Partners help people feel welcomed, encouraged, prayed for, and included. Partnership means taking an active part in the community you represent.

A few things to know

Common questions.

Have a question about your role, the community, or getting started? Come talk with the team in Discord.

Ask us in Discord
Who is the Creator Partnership Program for?

Christian streamers and gaming content creators who want to take part in a Christ-centered community, serve others, and grow alongside fellow creators. Twitch streamers, YouTube creators, and gaming video creators can explore the program with the team.

What do Creator Partners need to do?

Participate regularly in the FaithForge Discord, actively fulfill a volunteer role each calendar quarter, and create and interact in a way that reflects FaithForge’s community values.

Can I change my volunteer role?

Yes. You can choose a different role each quarter or continue in your current role. Work with the team to choose responsibilities that fit your availability and the community’s needs.

Is there a quarterly video or shorts quota?

There is no fixed quarterly video or shorts quota. Partnership requirements focus on active community participation and quarterly volunteer service.

When do my creator benefits begin?

The 18 community, support, resource, event, and growth benefits become available when you join. Your personal creator webpage and merchandise storefront begin at the start of your second calendar quarter as an active partner. Events follow their announced schedules, and free merchandise is an annual benefit.

How does the merchandise profit share work?

Once your merchandise storefront begins in your second calendar quarter, you keep 70% of the profit from your own merchandise. Discuss merchandise setup and payout details with the FaithForge team.

How do I become a Creator Partner?

Join the FaithForge Gaming Discord and ask the team about the Creator Partnership Program. Get to know the community, discuss your interests, and explore a volunteer role where you can contribute.
