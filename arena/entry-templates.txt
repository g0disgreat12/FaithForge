These examples are NOT loaded by the website. Copy one whole block into the
matching section of arena-data.js, replace the sample values, and commit.
Keep the comma after the closing brace. New IDs must be unique.

2. NEW MATCH — paste inside matches: [ ... ]
   Change the date, score, and opponent. Add the community above first.
   Result is calculated from your score; do not add a separate result field.

    {
      "id": "2027-01-16-new-community",
      "date": "2027-01-16",
      "gameId": "fortnite",
      "communityId": "new-community",
      "format": "Custom Games Tournament",
      "scoreType": "games",
      "scoreDirection": "higher",
      "ffgScore": 3,
      "opponentScore": 2,
      "tiedGames": 0,
      "description": "Write the match recap here.",
      "vodUrl": "",
      "image": "",
      "captainResults": [],
      "page": ""
    },

   Optional captainResults example (replace [] with a complete list):
   [{ "captainId": "doctorjsways", "wins": 3, "losses": 2, "ties": 0 }]
   Use actual results. For game-win scoring, captain totals must match the score.
