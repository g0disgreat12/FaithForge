About these figures

  • An active day has a message or at least one minute of shared voice. Shared voice is time with another non-deafened person; total voice includes solo and deafened time.
  • Activity helps guide a conversation; it does not automatically determine whether someone meets partnership expectations. Voice time measures presence, not attention or speaking.
  • Quarter dates use Eastern time and roll over automatically. Previous quarters remain selectable, subject to the tracker’s retention. Export a completed quarter to keep a copy.
  • Personal creator webpages and merchandise storefronts begin in a partner’s second calendar quarter; other program benefits begin when they join. See program details.
